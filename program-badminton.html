03 / DEVELOPMENT AREAS

What athletes
develop.

01

Racquet control

Grip changes, clean contact and stroke quality from defence to attack.

02

Footwork & coverage

Lunge, recover and split-step patterns that keep the court covered under pressure.

03

Shot selection

Choosing the right shot based on position, timing and opponent placement.

04

Match temperament

Patience in long rallies, focus between points and confidence in tight games.

05 / LEVELS & PROGRESSION

One pathway.
Three stages.

FOUNDATION

Start strong.

For athletes building basic badminton habits and enjoying structured training for the first time.

  • Grip & swing basics
  • Ladder footwork
  • Underarm rally play
DEVELOPMENT

Build range.

For athletes ready to handle more pressure, more decisions and greater technical demands.

  • Overhead strokes
  • Court coverage patterns
  • Singles & doubles tactics
PERFORMANCE

Raise the level.

For committed athletes preparing for stronger competition and higher training demands.

    07 / PROGRAM FAQ

    Before you
    start.

    Can a complete beginner join?

    Yes. Beginners can start in the Foundation pathway and build skills progressively.

    How is the right level decided?

    Age, experience, current ability and coach assessment are considered before placement.

    Does the program include match play?

    Conditioned rallies, target games and practice matches are used to apply skills in realistic situations.

    What should athletes bring?

    Appropriate sportswear, non-marking court shoes, a personally fitted racket if available, water and any academy-requested equipment.

    Can parents watch sessions?

    Viewing arrangements can vary by batch and academy schedule. Confirm with the academy team.

    Can athletes move between levels?

    Yes. Progression can be recommended by coaches when an athlete is ready for a higher challenge.
