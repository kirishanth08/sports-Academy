03 / COACHING APPROACH

How our coaches
work.

01

Observe first.

Coaches look at what an athlete can already do before deciding what to change. That keeps training relevant instead of forcing everyone through the same progression.

02

One clear focus.

Sessions are built around a small number of useful outcomes so athletes know what they are trying to improve and why it matters.

03

Pressure changes things.

Skills are gradually moved from isolated repetition into decisions, competition and realistic game situations.

04

Feedback should travel.

Coaches record useful observations so the next session can build from the previous one rather than starting over.

05 / COACHING METHOD

A session should
have a reason.

Our coaching framework gives athletes enough structure to know what they are learning while leaving enough room for coaches to adapt the challenge to the group.

01AssessIdentify current ability, movement quality and the most useful next challenge.
02TeachExplain and demonstrate the skill with simple cues athletes can remember.
03RepeatCreate enough quality repetitions for the movement or technique to become reliable.
04ChallengeAdd pressure, choices, movement or competition to test the skill.
05ReviewGive specific feedback and carry the next focus into future sessions.
